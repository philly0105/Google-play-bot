Clash Farmer PC
===============

Clash Farmer plays Clash of Clans for you in Google Play Games on this PC: it attacks and
collects loot while you do other things.

Read this first: using a bot breaks Supercell's terms of service, and Supercell can ban the
account for good. Only use it on an account you are ready to lose.


What you need
-------------
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. It was tested on Windows 11.
- A screen at least 900 pixels tall, such as 1920x1080. Clash's game area has to be at least
  720 pixels tall, and a 1366x768 laptop screen can't fit that.
- Google Play Games for PC, with Clash of Clans installed and signed in.
- Your Clash account linked to a Supercell ID. The bot recognises your account by the village
  name and tag in Clash's Settings, which show only for a linked account.
- Clash of Clans set to English (Settings > Language). The bot reads the game's text.


Install
-------
1. Right-click the zip, choose Properties, tick Unblock if it is there, and click OK.
2. Right-click the zip and choose Extract All. Put the folder anywhere you like, for example
   Documents. Don't run it from inside the zip.
3. Open the ClashFarmerPC folder and double-click ClashFarmerPC.exe.
4. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". The app isn't signed, so this is expected:
   click More info, then Run anyway. You only do this once.
   If Smart App Control blocks it with no Run anyway button, this PC can't run unsigned apps.
5. If you use Tailscale, Windows may ask whether to let the app through the firewall. Allowing
   it lets your other devices open the remote control page over Tailscale. Cancel is fine: the
   bot runs either way.


First run
---------
1. Open Clash of Clans in Google Play Games.
2. In Clash Farmer, open Setup and fix what it marks in red, and the amber lines you can fix.
   The Account and Calibration lines stay amber until the first supervised attack (step 4),
   and on Windows 10 the Windows line stays amber. Walls stays amber until you enter your
   storage sizes (see "While it plays").
3. Calibrate when Setup asks you to. Press "Start from the standard positions", then check
   each troop card against your own battle tray, press "Not in my army" on any hero or the jump
   spell if you don't have it, and press "Save calibration". The army needs one spell, such
   as Rage, for the bot to cast five times inside the base, so bring five of it: that card
   can't be left out. The card right of the main troop should not be another troop with a
   count of x5 or more, because the bot can take that for an event card in front of the tray.
4. Start one supervised attack and watch it all the way through before you leave it alone.

Closing the window keeps Clash Farmer running in the notification area (next to the clock; if
its icon isn't there, click the ^ arrow beside the clock). To stop it, right-click its icon and
choose Quit.


While it plays
--------------
- Keep the PC unlocked with the screen on. Locking it, or letting the screen turn off, stops
  the run, and Clash Farmer tells you why.
- You can use the PC meanwhile: Clash can sit behind other windows or be minimized, and the
  mouse and keyboard stay yours. Don't play Clash full screen.
- If Setup says the game is too small, do what it says: make Clash's window larger or maximize
  it, or move it to a larger screen. A maximized window is fine; only full screen is not.
- Don't close Clash's window while Google Play Games is still open. If you do, restart the PC
  before the next run: until then the game ignores the bot's taps.
- Don't open Clash on your phone or another device during a run. An unattended run then waits
  10 minutes and takes the village back, each time, until you stop it or its time limit ends
  it. A supervised attack just stops.
- If a run stops because Clash needs an update, update it in Google Play Games. If that closes
  Clash's window, restart the PC before the next run, as above.
- It never spends gems and never signs you in or out.
- To have runs stop once gold and elixir are full, enter your storage sizes: Setup, Edit
  settings, the capacity boxes. Without them a run keeps attacking until its limit.


Help
----
Setup has an "Open logs folder" button. If something goes wrong, send whoever gave you Clash
Farmer the newest clashfarmer-<date>.jsonl from that folder (what the run did) and desktop.log
(errors). If a run stopped on a problem, add the newest failure-*.png pictures from
%LOCALAPPDATA%\ClashFarmerPC\files\captures. These files show your village name, your player
tag and bits of the game's text.

To update, quit Clash Farmer, delete the old folder and extract the new zip. Your settings are
kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClashFarmerPC. To remove it completely, delete both.
